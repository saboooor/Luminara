## Set up a voice client

Install either **Plasmo Voice** or **Simple Voice Chat**, matching your Minecraft version and mod loader. You can join and play ordinary survival without a voice mod.

| Client            | Setup                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Plasmo Voice      | Press **V** by default, select your microphone and speakers, and choose push-to-talk or voice activation.     |
| Simple Voice Chat | Press **V** by default and follow its setup guide to select devices, activation mode, and a push-to-talk key. |

Check Minecraft’s keybind list if V conflicts with another mod. See the [Simple Voice Chat setup guide](https://modrepo.de/minecraft/voicechat/wiki/client_setup) for its onboarding steps.

Luminara's Voice Bridge lets nearby players using different voice mods hear one another. It bridges proximity conversation; private groups/channels are not bridged.

In Plasmo Voice, choose a voice range of **8, 16, or 32 blocks**, with **16 blocks** as the default. Simple Voice Chat and bridged audio follow their server distance settings. [Custom music discs](/docs/social/music-discs/) play through both Plasmo Voice and Simple Voice Chat, including jukebox controls, queues, and physical stereo speakers.

## Test with a friend

Choose a quiet location, set a comfortable volume, and test with someone nearby. Proximity voice depends on distance. Text chat can continue working while the separate voice connection is disconnected.

## Troubleshooting

Check the selected input/output devices and microphone permission in your operating system. Confirm you are not muted and are using the correct talk key. If the menu reports a connection error, record its exact text and your client version for support.

Voice is optional for normal survival. Do not assume unmodified Bedrock clients can install Java voice mods or hear the same custom audio.
