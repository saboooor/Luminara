## Pick the right vehicle

Available types include bicycles, cars, racing cars, sport bikes, hover bikes, trains, rafts, submarines, aircraft, tractors, drills, tanks, and mechas. Shop availability, model access, and allowed worlds can restrict what you can buy or use.

Start with `/whips help`. `/whips shop car` opens available car offers; `/whips shop bike sport` filters to that configured tier when available. Use tab completion for the actual vehicle and tier names.

## Buy from a showroom

Look directly at a displayed vehicle from nearby. A targeted display shows **Right-click to buy** below its tier and price. Right-click to buy that exact tier and material.

You need enough money, access to the offer, and a free inventory slot. The showroom model stays in place after purchase; it is not the vehicle you drive away. A missing or unavailable offer cannot be purchased just because its model is still displayed.

Check your inventory after buying and keep the vehicle item safe. Do not repeatedly click while checking whether a purchase succeeded.

## Place your purchase

Use the vehicle item in a suitable clear space in an allowed world. Put watercraft in water and trains on their intended rail route. Leave enough space for the model and its movement, not only the block you clicked.

Right-click your placed vehicle to mount. Ownership and model restrictions still apply after purchase. Ordinary players cannot take a showroom vehicle by mounting or picking it up.
