Vehicle upkeep has three separate parts. Choose the guide for what you need:

{/* audience:website */}

- [Refueling vehicles](/docs/vehicles/fuel/) — buy fuel, use the service screen, and check the gauge.
- [Cargo and storage](/docs/vehicles/storage/) — carry supplies and understand what remains with a packed vehicle.
- [Damage and repairs](/docs/vehicles/damage/) — recognize visual breakage and restore damaged models.

{/* /audience */}

## Before a long trip

Inspect fuel, check your cargo, and stop using a badly damaged vehicle. Pick a safe place to park before opening its service screen. Fuel, storage, and repair access can vary by vehicle type and world.

For movement controls, return to [driving](/docs/vehicles/driving/) or [aircraft](/docs/vehicles/aircraft/).
