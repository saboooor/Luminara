## Find a decoration

Use `/hdb` to open HeadDB, browse categories, or search with `/hdb search <query>`. Try a build theme such as “food” or “plants”. Results support filtering, sorting, and paging. Favorite heads you want to find again.

## Obtain and place heads

Choose a head through the menu, then place it as decoration. Availability and prices depend on server permissions and economy settings. Player heads have separate access rules. Server-created custom heads and categories may also appear; these remain local to the server.

## Access and help

Use `/hdb help` for available commands. If browsing or obtaining a head is denied, ask staff to check access. HeadDB provides `headdb.basic` for standard use, `headdb.category.<category-id>` for category access, and `headdb.admin` for administrators.

Staff can check `/hdb status`, refresh the catalog with `/hdb refresh`, or reload settings with `/hdb reload`. A verified cached catalog can remain available during an upstream outage.

This guide follows the [HeadDB resource](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/headdb.84967/). Consult the [official wiki](https://wiki.headsdb.com/) for version-specific administration.
