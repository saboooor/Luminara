## Open management

Sneak-right-click your own stone with an **empty main hand**. This opens management rather than applying a new body block. Ordinary visitors can discover and travel; ownership is needed to edit or retrieve a stone.

Type a name and choose **Close** to save it. Escape leaves the draft unsaved. On Bedrock, select Close in the action field and submit the form; simply dismissing it does not submit edits.

## Choose who can travel

| Access                      | Who can discover and use it?          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Local / allow other players | Players who visit and activate it     |
| Private                     | Only its owner                        |
| Global, if offered          | Everyone, without first activating it |

Use **Make private** or **Allow other players** in management. The command alternative is `/lumistones private <id> true` or `/lumistones private <id> false`. Turning off privacy restores access for players who discovered it earlier.

Private access also hides the name and runes from other players. It does not make the physical monument invisible or replace a land claim.

If **Make global** is offered, read its price before confirming. Returning a global stone to local access does not refund that price. Making a global stone private also removes its global status.

## Change its material

Sneak-right-click with a block in your main hand to apply that material to the body. Survival use consumes one block; applying the current material consumes nothing. The anchor remains a polished tuff wall. Changing appearance keeps the name, owner, and discoveries.

## Retrieve it

Mine an anchor to get the stone back as an item. Only its owner can normally retrieve it, and claim protection can still block breaking. In survival it drops nearby, so collect it. Creative breaking removes it without the survival item drop.

After moving a stone, check both arrival space and privacy before inviting visitors. See [travel basics](/docs/travel/waystones/) if someone cannot find it.
