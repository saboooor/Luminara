## Tractors

Tractors are intended for farming work. Test one on a small patch of your own mature crops before laying out a large field. Leave room for the machine to turn, keep its storage available, and inspect what was harvested and replanted.

A crop being inside the model does not necessarily mean it is eligible for harvesting. Crop type, growth state, vehicle tier, and protection rules can affect the result.

## Drills

Drills mine in front of the vehicle. Point the bit at a safe test area, move slowly, and stop before approaching another claim, a container room, or a dangerous opening.

The bit and body are separate materials: changing the body with a [wrap](/docs/vehicles/customization/) does not change the drill's tier or bit quality. Inspect the model's offer before buying a drill for a particular task.

## Tanks and mechas

These are specialist vehicles with additional abilities. Access to a model does not automatically grant every weapon or ability. Read `/whips help` and the server's activity rules before trying an action around players or builds.

## Before operating

Use an area where you have permission to change blocks. Machinery is not a way around a claim or public-region rule. Carry sufficient [fuel](/docs/vehicles/maintenance/), leave room for cargo, and test the relevant action before committing resources to a large project.
