## Catch special fish

Fish normally with a rod in an allowed location. Custom catches can have names, rarities, and sizes. Their availability can depend on location, biome, time, weather, or an active event.

Keep a special fish's item details intact. An ordinary renamed fish does not gain a custom catch's identity or value.

## Manage your collection

Inspect a catch before eating, trading, or selling it. Store favorite specimens separately. Bait, where available, can affect fishing; read its instructions and the fish requirements rather than assuming it guarantees a particular catch.

## Sell intentionally

Use `/emf shop` when fish selling is available. Inspect the selected sale option carefully: selling a single catch, selected fish, or everything eligible can have different results. Move keepsakes into storage before using broad sale actions.

## Why am I only catching ordinary loot?

Check that custom fishing is enabled for you and that the location meets the catch requirements. A rare catch is not guaranteed on every cast. Ordinary Minecraft fishing loot may still appear.

For timed rankings and event-specific catches, see [fishing competitions](/docs/progression/fishing-competitions/).
