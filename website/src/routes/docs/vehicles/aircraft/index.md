## Take off

Find a clear runway. Accelerate to takeoff speed, then look upward to climb. Looking downward produces descent. Pulling up before you have enough speed will not create lift. Leave room for wings, turns, and landing; avoid trying your first flight between buildings.

## Landing

Approach a clear landing area, reduce forward speed, and descend deliberately. Land before dismounting. Planes need [fuel](/docs/vehicles/maintenance/); running out at altitude can turn a normal trip into an emergency descent. Check fuel before every long flight.
