## Check your status

Use `/pvp` and read the response to see or change your available PvP state. Command details can vary, so follow its current usage message if it asks for an explicit state.

A personal combat preference works alongside world and region rules. An arena can have its own rules, and an area can prohibit combat regardless of your toggle.

## Arrange a fight

Agree with the other player before fighting and decide where the fight will happen. Keep spectators, builds, pets, and vehicle traffic out of the area. Do not use a toggle as a reason to harass someone or test damage on them without agreement.

## Limits of a toggle

Combat settings are not general invulnerability. Environmental hazards, falling, mobs, and feature-specific damage can still matter. A toggle can also have a cooldown or restrictions while you are in an activity.

Read the [rules](/rules/) and follow event instructions. If an interaction damages someone unexpectedly, stop testing it on players and report the location, combat state, and exact action.
