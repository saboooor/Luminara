## Stats

Open `/stats` to inspect your stat values and their effects. Skills can award stat levels as you progress. A stat's description explains what it changes; do not assume that a displayed number is a flat percentage bonus.

Skill progression can improve more than your tool output. Depending on the enabled stats, benefits can affect combat, health, toughness, luck, or mana-related play. Read the current menu before choosing a leveling goal.

## Passive abilities

Passive abilities belong to skills and unlock or improve at particular levels. They work under their stated conditions without the ready-and-activate sequence used by mana abilities.

An ability can have a chance to trigger, a material restriction, or another condition. A single harvest that does not trigger a bonus is not proof the ability is broken. Check its level and description first.

## Compare the right things

Your skill level, ability level, and stat level are different numbers. Raising one skill does not mean every ability gains a level immediately. Check the progression screen to see the next actual upgrade.

For effects you activate yourself, continue to [mana abilities](/docs/progression/mana-abilities/).
