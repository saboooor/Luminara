Waystones are physical monuments that form a travel network. Finding one is only the first step: **right-click its wall anchor to discover it for your character**.

## Discover a destination

Visit a stone and click its base. It becomes available in your travel menu, and its name and rune overlays become visible to you. You discover your own newly placed stone automatically. Another player's discovery does not unlock it for you.

Local stones require that first visit. A global stone, when global travel is available, can appear without discovery. An owner's private stone cannot be activated or used by anyone else, even if they visited it before it became private.

## Travel from a stone

1. Right-click the anchor to open the destination menu. Owners may need the Travel action in management.
2. Choose a destination you have access to.
3. Read the displayed travel cost and confirm the trip.
4. Stay still and avoid damage during the warmup.

Movement or damage interrupts warmup. Dismount and stop gliding before trying to travel. The destination needs enough headroom, safe ground nearby, and space inside the world border. A blocked landing, missing destination world, or cancelled teleport does not charge the normal travel cost.

XP costs use **levels**, not a fixed number of XP points. Money may also be charged if shown in the menu. The quoted cost is more useful than estimating from distance yourself.

## Travel without standing at a stone

Carry a [warp scroll](/docs/travel/warp-scrolls/). Command travel from anywhere is optional, so `/lumistones` is not a guaranteed replacement for visiting a stone or using a scroll. `/lumistones list` can show available destination names and IDs.

## Why is a destination missing?

You may not have activated it, its owner may have made it private, or travel between its world and yours may be unavailable. A natural waystone is discovered by clicking it too; passing a structure does not activate every stone inside.
