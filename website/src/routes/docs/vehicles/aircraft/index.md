## Choose an aircraft

| Vehicle    | Flight behavior                                                        |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Helicopter | Jump provides powered lift; without lift it descends                   |
| Broom      | Jump provides powered lift; releasing it allows descent                |
| Plane      | Needs forward speed, then uses your looking pitch for climb or descent |
| Parachute  | Glides while losing height rather than providing powered ascent        |

Use your configured jump and movement controls. Aircraft availability and altitude limits depend on the world and vehicle.

## Helicopters and brooms

Begin in a clear area with open space overhead. Hold jump to gain height while powered. A helicopter needs to lift off before building forward speed. Releasing the lift input causes descent, so do not treat the vehicle as a stationary flying platform that will hold altitude for you.

## Planes

Find a clear runway. Accelerate to takeoff speed, then look upward to climb. Looking downward produces descent. Pulling up before you have enough speed will not create lift. Leave room for wings, turns, and landing; avoid trying your first flight between buildings.

## Parachutes

A parachute gives you a controlled descent, not a way to climb back to a launch point. Plan where you can land before committing to a long glide.

## Landing

Approach a clear landing area, reduce forward speed, and descend deliberately. Land before dismounting. Powered aircraft need [fuel](/docs/vehicles/maintenance/); running out at altitude can turn a normal trip into an emergency descent. Check fuel before every long flight.
