## Enter a flight area

Designated areas can grant flight while you are inside their boundaries. Look for the area's instructions and any entry message. When flight is granted, use your normal flight controls; Java players normally double-tap jump to begin flying.

A flight zone has both horizontal and vertical limits. Flying high above its marker can leave the zone even when you are still over the same ground.

## Leaving the boundary

Flight granted by the area ends when you leave it. Land before crossing the edge, especially over water, cliffs, or the void. Any fall-damage grace period is temporary and should not be treated as permanent protection.

Overlapping eligible zones keep area flight active until you leave all of them. Flight you already had from your game mode or another entitlement is handled separately.

## If you cannot fly

Check that you are inside the marked area and not above its ceiling. Access can depend on the area and your account. A public flight zone does not grant permission to build, enter private land, or fly throughout survival.

For travel beyond the area, use [waystones](/docs/travel/waystones/) or [aircraft](/docs/vehicles/aircraft/).
