## Rafts

Place a raft in a sufficiently open body of water. Forward input builds speed, backward input slows it, and left/right turns. Surface buoyancy keeps it near the waterline. Trying to accelerate on dry ground will not work like driving a car.

Leave turning room at docks. Slow down before reaching the bank, then stop before dismounting or picking it up.

## Submarines

Submarines propel themselves in water. Jump provides powered ascent; without that input they descend. Monitor your surroundings and fuel before travelling beneath an overhang or deep water.

If a special camera mode is active, movement can be locked while you use it. Exit that mode before expecting normal steering and propulsion. Practice near a safe shore before taking a long underwater trip.

## Trains

Trains follow the rails' shape, including curves and slopes. Build or use a continuous route with enough clearance. They are not free-steering road vehicles: turning the controls cannot replace a missing or incorrectly connected rail.

Powered rails can affect movement when enabled. If the train stops at the same point each time, inspect the track, slope, and power rather than only holding forward longer. Do not modify another player's rail network without permission.

## Before a trip

Check [fuel, damage, and storage](/docs/vehicles/maintenance/). World and ownership restrictions apply to these vehicles too. Keep an ordinary return route available while learning unfamiliar controls.
