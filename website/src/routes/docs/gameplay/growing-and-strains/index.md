## Browse strains

Use `/strains` to inspect the available varieties. Strain identity, rarity, and growth behavior can affect the resulting consumable. Check a variety's details rather than assuming every crop produces an interchangeable ingredient.

Follow the current planting and harvesting instructions from the menu or item. Allow time for growth and inspect the harvested result before expanding a farm.

## Item forms and tools

Bongs and carts have their own usage, cooldown, and durability rules. A reusable-looking item is not necessarily unlimited. Check its remaining uses and the ingredient it accepts before expecting repeated activation.

## Farm controllers and workers

Where available, farm controllers and worker villagers automate parts of the cultivation workflow. Start with a small test plot, follow the controller's interaction instructions, and check worker assignment and available inputs/output space.

Keep farms on land where you have building permission. Automation does not grant ownership of another player's crops or villagers.

## Progress and effects

Use Minecraft's Advancements screen for the consumable system's achievements; `/drugs achievements` points you to that progression. Use [status and tolerance checks](/docs/gameplay/tolerance-and-recovery/) separately to understand the effects on your character.
