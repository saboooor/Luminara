## Sit or pose

Use `/sit` to sit where allowed. `/lay` and `/crawl` provide other poses when available. Empty-hand right-clicking a suitable stair or slab can also create a seat.

Use your normal dismount or the pose's exit control to stand again. Leave enough space to exit safely, particularly beside a wall or drop.

If right-click seating gets in your way, `/sit toggle` changes that interaction when available. Player-stacking has its own `/sit playertoggle` control and access rules; do not assume everyone wants to be used as a seat.

## See interactions in the world

Crafting and container-related activity can have visual item displays. These help show what is happening without requiring everyone to open the same screen. They do not by themselves transfer ownership of the displayed items.

For a deliberately arranged chest-content display, use [shelves](/docs/building/shelf-displays/). For wall artwork, see [image maps](/docs/building/images-and-maps/).

## Respect shared spaces

Posing does not bypass land restrictions or reserve a location. Avoid blocking a shop sign, portal entrance, or another player's interaction target while using a social pose.
