## Check the next event

Use `/emf next` to see upcoming competition information when available. `/emf help` lists your accessible commands. Timing and rewards follow the server's current schedule.

## Read the objective

Competitions can score different things: the largest fish, most fish, total size, or a specific catch. A big fish does not automatically win an event based on catch count.

At the start, read the announcement and progress display. Fish in an eligible area during the active window, and use the event's standings display or available leaderboard command to check progress.

## After the timer

Wait for the result announcement. Keep space for item rewards and follow the displayed reward instructions. Catches outside the event window or its requirements may not count.

If the display is hidden, check `/emf toggle` for your available visibility controls. Hiding a progress display is different from changing whether your catches qualify.
