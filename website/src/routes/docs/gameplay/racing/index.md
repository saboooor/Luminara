## Join a track

Use the track's posted join signs or lobby instructions. For ice-boat racing, the standard player commands are `/iceboatracing join <trackName>`, `/iceboatracing lobby`, and `/iceboatracing leave` where exposed by the installed racing edition. Use the exact track name from its sign or menu.

Track capacity, an active race, or an entry fee can stop a join. Read the response instead of trying to force a start.

## Drive the course

Ice boats preserve momentum. Begin steering before a bend and avoid large late corrections. Learn one track at low speed, then focus on a repeatable line through its checkpoints.

Kart activities use their own lobby and vehicle instructions. Read those controls before the countdown; a racing kart is not necessarily controlled or purchased like your personal [road vehicle](/docs/vehicles/driving/).

## Finish or leave

Follow checkpoint order and complete the required laps. Cutting a section can leave a lap uncounted. Use the activity's leave control to exit so it can return you through the intended flow.

Rewards, cosmetic choices, and records depend on the specific activity. Check the current track display rather than assuming every race pays money.
