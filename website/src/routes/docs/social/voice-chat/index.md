## Set up a voice client

Use **Plasmo Voice**, matching your Minecraft version and mod loader. You can join and play without the voice mod.

Press **V** by default to open the Plasmo Voice menu. Choose your microphone and speakers, configure push-to-talk or voice activation, and check Minecraft’s keybind list if another mod uses V.

Choose a voice range of **8, 16, or 32 blocks**, with **16 blocks** as the default. [Custom music discs](/docs/social/music-discs/) also require compatible Plasmo Voice playback.

## Test with a friend

Choose a quiet location, set a comfortable volume, and test with someone nearby. Proximity voice depends on distance. Text chat can continue working while the separate voice connection is disconnected.

## Troubleshooting

Check the selected input/output devices and microphone permission in your operating system. Confirm you are not muted and are using the correct talk key. If the menu reports a connection error, record its exact text and your client version for support.

Voice is optional for normal survival. Do not assume unmodified Bedrock clients can install Java voice mods or hear the same custom audio.
